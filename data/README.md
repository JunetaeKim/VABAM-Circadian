# Data

Raw files are kept as obtained and never modified. Only the primary dataset is included in the repository; place the others at the paths below.

## Included

### `raw/msb2025_breast_cancer/raw_lumicycle_data.xlsx`

Ector C. et al. *Circadian clock features define novel subtypes among breast cancer cells and shape drug sensitivity.* Mol Syst Biol 21, 315–340 (2025). doi:10.1038/s44320-025-00092-7. Figshare item [28212131](https://figshare.com/articles/dataset/Fig2_circadian_signal_traces_and_final_parameters/28212131), CC BY 4.0. Copy taken from [Granada-Lab/Circadian-Subtypes-Breast-Cancer-Cells](https://github.com/Granada-Lab/Circadian-Subtypes-Breast-Cancer-Cells) (`Figure2_deep_circadian_phenotyping/Fig2_1_python/`).

- `raw_truncated`: `Time` (h) + 180 traces, 5.00–142.67 h, 827 rows, 10-min sampling (default input).
- `raw_full`: 0–206 h, NaN-padded (lengths differ between experiments).
- `legend`: column naming `date_cellline[_genotype]_reporter[_Luc][_sync][_replicate]`; `BLH` = Bmal1-Luc; synchronisation is 1 µM dexamethasone unless marked `15uMDe`/`15Dex` (1.5 µM) or `serumshock`.
- U-2 OS: WT Bmal1 ×6, WT Per2 ×6, CRY1 sKO, CRY2 sKO and CRY1/2 dKO Bmal1 ×6 each (2022-06-28 and 2022-07-05, three replicates per day).
- `20211102_MDAMB468_Per2_2` ends at 134.67 h; only the finite part is used.

Notes

- Nat Commun 2024 vs MSB 2025: 135 of 164 traces are value-identical to MSB `raw_full` with a +6.0 h time offset (`python run.py audit`); only the 29 new traces (GIMEN, SH-SY5Y) are used, as external set E2.
- Circa-SCOPE: NIH-3T3, Rev-VNP fluorescence, single-cell tracks at 1 h for 9 days, drug or serum added at 108 h. No header; rows = time points, columns = cells. Well and experiment identifiers are not provided, so cells are the only reportable independent unit.
- HES1: MCF7 mVenus-Hes1 at 0.25–0.5 h; only the 26 tracks ≥ 66.6 h are used.

`data/processed/` is regenerated by `python run.py preprocess` and `python run.py multi`.
